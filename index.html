Our Story

Born in Watamu,
Passionate About Kenya

I'm Peter Wesonga, and Watamu is not just where I work — it's my home. Growing up along the turquoise shores of the Kenyan coast, surrounded by mangroves, coral reefs, and the call of the African bush, I fell in love with every corner of this extraordinary country.

After years of guiding international visitors through Kenya's most breathtaking landscapes — from the Maasai Mara to Tsavo, from Watamu Marine Park to Arabuko-Sokoke Forest — I founded Watamu Wilderness Safaris to share this magic on my own terms: authentically, responsibly, and with heart.

My Story

Born Here.
Passionate Always.

I'm Peter Wesonga — born and raised on the turquoise shores of Watamu, a small coastal village in Kilifi County, Kenya. Growing up, the Indian Ocean was my playground, the Arabuko-Sokoke Forest my backyard, and Tsavo's vast red plains my dream horizon.

From a young age, I would guide curious visitors along our beaches, pointing out sea turtles, identifying coastal birds, and translating the rhythms of Giriama village life. That spark never left me. After completing my formal guiding certification with the Kenya Tourism Board, I spent years guiding across Kenya — from Amboseli's elephant herds beneath Kilimanjaro to the dramatic flamingo-fringed shores of Lake Nakuru.

In 2012, I founded Watamu Wilderness Safaris with a single conviction: the best Kenya experiences are led by people who truly love this land. Not outsiders reading from a script, but locals who know where the lions sleep, which tides bring the dolphins, and which elders will welcome you into their home.

Today, I lead small-group tours and custom itineraries that combine adventure, culture and conservation — always honouring the communities and ecosystems that make Kenya extraordinary.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

For coastal day tours, 1–3 days' notice is usually fine. For multi-day safaris like Maasai Mara, we recommend 2–4 weeks ahead, especially July–October (migration season).

What's included in the tour prices?

Prices include park fees, guide fees, transport, and meals as listed per tour. International flights and travel insurance are not included. We'll be clear about everything upfront.

Do you offer family-friendly tours?

Absolutely! Many of our tours are perfect for families. Peter will adjust the pace, activities and accommodation to suit children of all ages.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, PayPal and major credit cards. A 30% deposit secures your booking, with the balance due 2 weeks before travel.
